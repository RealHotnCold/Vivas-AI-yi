# Research Notes

The project direction is supported by research showing that ENSO impacts agriculture in crop- and geography-specific ways and that satellite/weather data can support monitoring and prediction.

https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms4712

https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-017-02071-5
