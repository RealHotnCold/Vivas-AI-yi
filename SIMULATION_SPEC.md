# Simulation Specification

## Purpose

Propagate a climate shock through an agricultural system.

## Scenario design

Prefer historical ENSO analogs and empirically observed regional relationships over arbitrary percentage shocks.

Scenario metadata should include:
- ENSO state/intensity
- analog years or calibration period
- region
- season
- variables perturbed
- source/calibration

## Pipeline

Climate scenario
→ rainfall/temperature
→ water availability
→ crop stress
→ yield
→ production
→ economic impact

## Outputs

- climate anomaly
- water stress
- crop stress
- yield anomaly
- production change
- economic impact
- uncertainty/limitations
- provenance

## Important

If a relationship is uncertain, expose the uncertainty rather than presenting a precise-looking fake number.
